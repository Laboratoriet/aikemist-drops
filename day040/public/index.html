WCAG 2.1 Requirements

  • AA Normal: 4.5:1 — The legal minimum for most jurisdictions
  • AA Large: 3:1 — For text ≥18pt (or ≥14pt bold)
  • AAA Normal: 7:1 — Enhanced readability
  • AAA Large: 4.5:1 — Enhanced for large text

Most accessibility lawsuits cite WCAG AA as the standard. AAA is aspirational but not legally required. When in doubt, hit 4.5:1 minimum.
